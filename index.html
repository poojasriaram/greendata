01 — GreenNext Platform

Infrastructure designed for the next generation of digital growth.

MASTER PLANNING · CO-DEVELOPMENT · INSTITUTIONAL CAPITAL ALIGNMENT

GreenNext Technologies is a strategic digital infrastructure development platform focused on curating, master-planning, and executing landmark Data Center, AI/GPU Compute, and Integrated IT Park Campuses in South India.

Moving beyond traditional commercial real estate, GreenNext creates interconnected Technology & Digital Infrastructure Business Districts. By combining high-density power corridors, dual-grid utility feeds, carrier-neutral telecom backbones, and Global Capability Center (GCC) workspaces, we position Tamil Nadu's secondary economic hubs as premier national computing destinations.

Our strategic land platform in Coimbatore and Madurai offers developers, global operators, and institutional investors ready-to-scale canvases with streamlined statutory frameworks, deep technical talent access, and lower total cost of occupancy.

05 — Strategic Advantages

Why Coimbatore & Madurai. Why Now.

Diversifying away from congested primary metro hubs (Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi NCR) through superior power availability, lower cost of occupancy, and robust institutional backing.

01 / POWER

Robust Power Infrastructure

Access to dedicated high-voltage TANGEDCO substations, dual-grid feeder lines, renewable energy wheeling pathways, and battery energy storage system (BESS) integration.

02 / FIBER

High-Speed Digital Connectivity

Diverse dark-fiber routes connecting directly to Chennai and Mumbai landing stations, carrier-neutral meet-me rooms, and ultra-low latency regional PoPs.

03 / ECOSYSTEM

Established Tech Ecosystem

Coimbatore hosts TIDEL Park (~1.7M sq.ft.) and Vilankurichi ELCO SEZ (Wipro, Access Healthcare). Madurai benefits from established SIPCOT/ELCOT industrial frameworks.

04 / TALENT

Deep Engineering Talent Pool

Surrounded by premier universities (including Thiagarajar College of Engineering, PSG Tech), yielding steady pipelines of electrical, HVAC, cloud, and network engineers.

05 / LAND

Scalable Land Strategy

Clear-title, large-scale contiguous parcels ranging from 3 to 50 acres, providing master-plan flexibility to phase capacity alongside customer demand.

06 / VALUE

Superior ROI & Regional Reach

Significantly lower real estate acquisition and operating overhead compared to Tier-1 metros, with direct highway/air transit across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, and AP.

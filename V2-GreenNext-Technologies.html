05 — Market Outlook

The India Data Center market outlook

India’s Data Center sector is entering a sustained, high-growth phase driven by cloud adoption, generative AI, data localization, and enterprise digitization.

By 2030 4–12 GW Projected capacity Sustained capacity build-out across India’s data center market. CareEdge · Colliers · Savills · Wood Mackenzie
By 2035 ~13.8 GW Installed capacity ~US$71.6B in direct investment — a ~US$280B ecosystem. PwC India
By 2047 ~65 GW IT load ~394 TWh of electricity demand. NITI Aayog

Strategic rationale

With AI driving the shift to high-density racks (30–100kW+) requiring liquid cooling and advanced networking, power and location are the defining competitive advantages. Our Coimbatore and Madurai campuses are perfectly positioned to capture this demand.

  1. 2030 Cloud & enterprise workloads
  2. 2035 AI / GPU / HPC infrastructure
  3. 2045+ Sovereign digital infrastructure
